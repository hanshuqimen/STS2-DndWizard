# Assets and attribution

37 original card illustrations and the character sprite were generated specifically for this project with the built-in image_gen tool. Original images are in art-source/illustrations and art-source/wizard-original.png. The retained generation records document the prompts and their project-relative output files.

Runtime card textures are 512×384. The character sprite retains transparency. UI, energy, power and relic icons are authored SVG geometry, generated and rasterized by the included tools.

Game audio and some base-game scenes are referenced at runtime. Their files are not copied into this repository or release. BaseLib must be obtained separately from its own distribution and is subject to its own license. Reference mods were studied for architecture; their source and media are not included here.
